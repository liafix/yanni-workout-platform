# YANNI WORKOUT PLATFORM
## PHASE 01 — Master Product & Creative Brief

**Status:** APPROVED FOUNDATION / ready for Phase 02  
**Project type:** High-ticket creator fitness platform — interactive demo  
**Primary language:** Slovak  
**Primary brand:** YANNI WORKOUT  
**Premium membership:** YWC / YANNI WORKOUT CLUB  
**Demo presentation:** 9 August 2026, Trenčín  
**Primary presentation devices:** iPhone + notebook  
**Production status:** Demo / concept. Not presented as a finished production platform.

---

## 1. Executive thesis

YANNI WORKOUT should become the digital home of Yanni’s fitness brand: one premium platform that connects content, challenges, coaching, community, affiliate partnerships, training products and YWC membership into a coherent fan and revenue ecosystem.

The demo must make this idea understandable within the first 10–15 seconds.

The project is not a replacement for every current service on day one. The first version acts as a premium orchestration layer over Yanni’s existing ecosystem. Existing services may continue to handle coaching forms, membership/content, podcast distribution, downloads and partner purchases until a production migration is justified.

### Core product sentence

> **YANNI WORKOUT je miesto, kde fanúšik sleduje Yanniho, trénuje s ním, zapája sa do výziev a získava prístup k jeho prémiovému svetu.**

### Business sentence

> Platforma spája Yanniho pozornosť, obsah a existujúce ponuky do jedného vlastného funnelu, ktorý môže generovať príjem cez členstvo, coaching, digitálne produkty, affiliate, eventy, merch a partnerstvá.

## 2. Problem / opportunity

Yanni already has valuable pieces of a creator business, but the visitor experience is distributed across separate destinations. A fan arriving from social media must decide among external links without seeing one coherent brand story, product hierarchy or progression from free follower to paying customer.

The opportunity is to create one owned destination that answers four questions quickly:

1. **Kto je Yanni a prečo ho mám sledovať?**
2. **Čo môžem urobiť práve teraz?**
3. **Ako môžem s Yannim trénovať alebo sa dostať bližšie ku komunite?**
4. **Čo si môžem kúpiť / predplatiť / rezervovať bez hľadania ďalších odkazov?**

The platform should convert passive attention into repeated participation.

## 3. Primary audiences

### A. Fanúšik / follower

**Context:** arrives from Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, event QR or direct recommendation. Often on mobile.  
**Needs:** see latest content, understand what Yanni offers, find an event, use a discount code, join a premium experience.  
**Primary next actions:** Watch / Challenge / YWC / Gear.

### B. Ambitious gym-goer

**Context:** actively wants a better training plan, technique, accountability or coaching.  
**Needs:** clear path from inspiration to a concrete fitness product.  
**Primary next actions:** Program / Coaching / YWC.

### C. Challenge participant

**Context:** arrives before, during or after an event.  
**Needs:** event details, registration, rules, result, ranking, media, next event.  
**Primary next actions:** Register / View leaderboard / Join next challenge.

### D. High-intent premium member

**Context:** follows Yanni regularly and wants access beyond public social content.  
**Needs:** exclusive content, program access, early access, member benefits and identity/status.  
**Primary next action:** Join YWC.

### E. Brand / sponsor / business partner

**Context:** evaluates Yanni as a creator, athlete, host or event partner.  
**Needs:** professional presentation, partnership formats, event potential and contact path.  
**Primary next action:** Work with Yanni.

---

## 4. Visitor modes

The platform deliberately contains two different interface modes.

### Public website: PERSUADE + EXPERIENCE

Goal: make Yanni’s world desirable, understandable and actionable. The website can be expressive, cinematic and editorial, but every visual moment must support a real action or product truth.

### Member / challenge tools: OPERATE

Goal: allow the user to complete tasks quickly: see a program, find progress, view an event, check a leaderboard, manage membership. Here clarity beats spectacle.

This split prevents an Awwwards-style homepage from turning the entire product into an unusable showpiece.

## 5. Primary product outcome

### For Yanni

Create one ownable digital destination that can:

- increase conversion from social attention to paid offers,
- make current offers easier to discover,
- strengthen the perceived value of YANNI WORKOUT as a brand,
- give events and challenges a permanent digital layer,
- create new recurring and one-time revenue opportunities,
- make sponsorship and collaboration inventory easier to package,

### For the fan

One place to:

- discover Yanni’s current content,
- see and join challenges,
- access training offers,
- apply for coaching,
- use partner recommendations and codes,
- join YWC,
- later build a member identity, progress and challenge history.

## 6. Product pillars

### 6.1 WATCH — Obsah

A curated content hub for latest / selected Reels, YouTube content and YanniCast.

**Purpose:** retain attention and demonstrate expertise/personality.  
**Demo:** curated cards and editorial content presentation.  
**Production later:** automated content ingestion where technically appropriate.

### 6.2 TRAIN — Tréning

Programs, exercise education and coaching entry point.

**Purpose:** turn fitness intent into a paid or high-intent action.  
**Demo:** one flagship program + coaching landing flow + current external links.  
**Production later:** owned program library, access control, progress.

### 6.3 CHALLENGES — Súťaže a eventy

Upcoming event, registration, rules, countdown, results and leaderboard concept.

**Purpose:** transform offline events into repeatable digital community moments.  
**Demo anchor:** Trenčín Biceps Challenge.  
**Production later:** event CMS, registration, participant profiles, verified results, season/tour history.

### 6.4 YWC — Premium klub

Premium membership layer for exclusive content, early access, programs, benefits and member identity.

**Purpose:** recurring revenue and deeper fan relationship.  
**Demo:** membership landing + concept member dashboard.  
**Initial action:** current YWC / existing service may remain the destination until production migration is agreed.

### 6.5 GEAR — Affiliate / partner ecosystem

A curated “Yanni používa / odporúča” destination with current partner code and future affiliate products.

**Purpose:** monetize product intent without forcing a full store.  
**Demo:** premium gear module, current promo code, external partner CTA.  
**Production later:** tracked affiliate links and performance analytics.

### 6.6 YANNI — Story & achievements

Cinematic athlete/creator story, milestones, selected achievements, event history and brand evolution.

**Purpose:** brand authority, emotional connection and sponsor credibility.  
**Rule:** only use verified milestones and claims.

### 6.7 PARTNERS — Work with Yanni

Professional collaboration surface for brands, sponsors and event partners.

**Purpose:** open a B2B revenue path separate from fan monetization.  
**Demo:** collaboration formats + contact CTA, without invented client metrics.

## 7. Revenue architecture

Phase 01 identifies revenue mechanisms but does not invent performance claims or prices.

### Tier 1 — Existing / immediately routeable

1. **Online coaching** — route to current application/service.
2. **YWC premium membership** — route to current membership destination where appropriate.
3. **Affiliate / GymBeam code and links** — surface more prominently and contextually.
4. **Existing content / podcast ecosystem** — supports trust, retention and cross-platform growth.

### Tier 2 — Buildable production revenue

5. **One-time training programs** — Stripe Checkout / owned access.
6. **Challenge registration / paid entry** — Stripe when event model supports paid participation.
7. **Premium event access / early registration** — YWC benefit or paid tier.
8. **Merch drops** — limited release commerce rather than an oversized generic store.
9. **Sponsor inventory** — challenge partner, podcast partner, content integration, event sponsor.

### Tier 3 — Platform expansion

10. **Owned recurring YWC billing** — Stripe Billing when migration is justified.
11. **Member upsells** — program bundles, coaching application, event priority.
12. **Challenge ecosystem** — city events, rankings, profiles, seasonal participation.
13. **Digital workshops / masterclasses** — premium one-time content.

A dedicated ROI document in Phase 03 will model Conservative / Base / Upside scenarios using explicit assumptions.

## 8. Current-service integration rule

The demo must respect Yanni’s current business rather than pretending external services do not exist.

### Rule

If an existing service already performs a real job, the demo can act as the premium front-end and route the visitor there.

Examples:

- challenge registration → existing Google Form,
- coaching / Yanni Leto → current Typeform or current destination,
- training plan → current download/delivery destination,
- YanniCast → Spotify,
- YWC → current premium platform,
- partner purchase → current affiliate destination.

### Production principle

Only migrate a function into the owned platform when the migration improves economics, experience, data ownership or operational control enough to justify added complexity.

Without explanation, Yanni should understand:

1. this is his brand,
2. this is larger than a normal personal website,
3. Challenges and YWC are core products,

- hero / brand,
- Trenčín challenge,
- YWC,
- coaching/program,
- Gear / code,
- one member/dashboard or leaderboard moment.

### Mobile test

The mobile version must feel intentionally designed, not compressed from desktop.

### Notebook test

The larger viewport must provide the cinematic “high-ticket” impact and make the motion/art direction visible.

### Credibility test

No visitor should mistake a simulated backend feature, fabricated metric or speculative revenue model for a verified live capability.

## 17. Product risks and mitigations

### Risk: too much scope

**Mitigation:** vertical slice. Finish a smaller set of surfaces to a high standard and represent deeper systems as clearly labelled concepts.

### Risk: visual spectacle hides the offer

**Mitigation:** every major section gets one clear user action. Homepage remains Persuade/Experience; tools use Operate principles.

### Risk: generic AI design

**Mitigation:** derive design from Yanni’s real photography, events, training objects and creator identity; enforce a design constitution before code.

### Risk: misleading business claims

**Mitigation:** verified facts only. ROI uses assumptions and scenarios, not promises.

### Risk: poor mobile performance

**Mitigation:** mobile-first performance budget, selective motion, reduced-motion path, optimized assets and no unnecessary full-site WebGL.

### Risk: external-service fragmentation remains

**Mitigation:** owned platform becomes the front door first; migration becomes a later business decision.
